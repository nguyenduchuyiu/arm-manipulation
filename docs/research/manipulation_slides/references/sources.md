The workspace pipeline is implemented in `OATFlow/data_tools/voxel_grid.py`. It uses SciPy bounded least-squares IK with a MuJoCo Jacobian, a local implementation of RRT-Connect, and MuJoCo collision/dynamics checks. The arm/table scene is the retained user configuration.

The workspace figure and web GLBs use the original visual STL meshes from `robotics_assets/robot/nexarm/meshes/`, transformed using MuJoCo forward kinematics at the recorded settled home. Frozen visual geometry and source metadata are in `data/arm_visual_home.{npz,json}`. The exact accepted voxel boundary is rendered with transparency; all 32,017 centers are supplied in `assets3d/voxel_centers.f32`. The original triangle geometry is preserved in `arm_home_original.glb`; the web variant is decimated. No geometry is generated by an image model.

## TCOW attribution

Basile Van Hoorick, Pavel Tokmakov, Simon Stent, Jie Li, and Carl Vondrick. **Tracking through Containers and Occluders in the Wild.** CVPR 2023.

Official project and paper links: [TCOW project page](https://tcow.cs.columbia.edu/).

Original TCOW predicts target, occluder, and container segmentation. Our adaptation uses its causal RGB/query backbone with an initial point prompt and an XYZ head. These adaptations and the robot-control results are project experiments, not claims from the TCOW paper.

## Interpretation

- Reaching results use one learned model with two inference-noise seeds, not two independent training runs.
- Grasp success requires the correct target to rise by more than 4 cm with bilateral jaw contact for 10 consecutive policy frames.
- The 3,000-update auxiliary-loss comparison is separate from the 10-epoch FM-weight-initialization comparison.
- The 92.2% result includes training cases; held-out success is 86/96 = 89.6%.
- Layout generalization is within the sampled position range, with known objects and one fixed camera.
- e105 has a stricter full-task criterion that includes correct cover deposit and limited other-cover displacement. Its result fields are blank.
- Quantitative standalone tracker tables remain omitted. Two qualitative e100 videos have been added at the user’s request, labeled as tracking-pilot step 1,250; exact source/checkpoint metadata are in videos/metadata.json.
